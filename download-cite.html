Citing an individual variant entry

Each variant's underlying finding, whether a case report, functional assay, or population survey, belongs to the researchers who published it. When you use a specific variant record, cite both:

  1. The original publication(s) listed in that variant's Key reference field (with its PubMed link, when available), for the scientific finding itself.
  2. This database, for the compilation, ISBT cross-referencing, gnomAD/TOPMed annotation, and base-editing feasibility assessment.

Every variant's detail panel (open any row in the browser) includes a ready-made citation line combining both, which you can copy directly.

Example: Cai X, et al. [original citation from the record's Key reference field]. Cited in: Blood Group Non-Coding Variant Database, variant ABO-NC-001 (ABO). Compiled by Jun Liu, MD, PhD (Chai Lab, Brigham and Women's Hospital / Harvard Medical School).

About this resource

A research catalog of non-coding and regulatory blood group variants, compiled for the study of variant-guided base editing strategies for antigen modulation. Not a clinical or diagnostic tool.
