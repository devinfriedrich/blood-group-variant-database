Changelog

2026-08-14: NIH-convention & domain-accuracy audit

Ran two independent audits: a website-design pass comparing the site's text and structure against NCBI/NIH resource-page conventions (ClinVar's "What is ClinVar?", the NCBI variation guide), and a blood group genetics domain pass checking terminology and factual framing against ISBT nomenclature. Changes made as a result:

  • Fixed a real inconsistency: the site called itself both "6 blood group systems" (hero lede) and "8 blood group systems" (stat tile) at once. RHD/RHCE are two genes within one ISBT system (RH, 004), not two systems; Kpa/Kpb and Jsa/Jsb are antigens within KEL (006), not an eighth system. The site now consistently states 6 ISBT systems, with RH and KEL browsable by their component genes/antigen groupings for guide-design convenience, explained where that split first appears.
  • Data fixes: the Kell-null phenotype was written "K0" (numeral) on 4 KellOther records vs. "Ko" (letter) on all 12 KEL records; standardized to "Ko". RHD-NC-001 had an internal AI-scoring/policy-review note sitting in the alternative_notation field (meant for legacy nomenclature); moved it into molecular_mechanism as a bracketed audit note. Gene count corrected from 7 to 8 (GYPA/GYPB was being counted as one gene).
  • New content: a "What is the Blood Group Variant Database?" opening statement and an FAQ section (About), a field-by-field data dictionary and a formal References section (Methods), and an NLM/Citing-Medicine-format citation (Citation) in place of the ad hoc "Available at... Accessed..." version.
  • Tone/formatting: converted the hero's CTA buttons and disclaimer pill badge to plain hyperlinks and a trailing sentence (NCBI resource pages use plain links, not marketing-style buttons); flattened the Methods disclaimer from a tinted callout box into a plain paragraph; fixed a "Stats"/"Statistics" nav-label mismatch and a "Validation evidence tier"/"Validation tier" naming inconsistency; added a visible data-release date to Downloads and Methods.

2026-08-13: Full citation audit + site expansion

Cross-checked all 88 unique PMIDs cited across all 229 variants (141 citation segments) against live PubMed metadata, expanding on the prior sample audit. Found and corrected 15 additional citation errors beyond the original 4, and flagged 3 more that could not be confidently resolved by search alone:

  • Corrected (15): ACKR1-NC-004, KEL-NC-006, KEL-NC-007, RHCE-NC-003, RHCE-NC-014, RHCE-NC-018, RHCE-NC-007, RHCE-NC-023, ACKR1-NC-003, KEL-NC-008, KEL-NC-011, MNS-NC-016, MNS-NC-022, ABO-NC-012, KELLOTHER-NC-008: wrong author, wrong journal, wrong year, or (in three cases) a PMID pointing to an entirely unrelated paper, in each case replaced with a verified correct reference.
  • Flagged for manual follow-up (3): ABO-NC-036 (was incorrectly marked "VERIFIED"; its PMID resolves to an unrelated neurology paper), one of two references each on ACKR1-NC-002 and KELLOTHER-NC-010. Left as unresolved rather than guessed.

Also added: the Statistics, Downloads, About, and this Changelog page; a working mobile navigation menu; CSV export regenerated automatically alongside the JSON on every data change.

2026-08-12: Initial site build + sample citation audit

Rebuilt the site from the original data compilation: normalized the eight per-system source tables into one shared schema, replaced the prior generic branding with proper Chai Lab / Jun Liu attribution, and designed the searchable browser, Methods, and Citation pages.

Spot-checked a stratified sample of 24 variants (2-3 per system) against PubMed. Found 4 citation errors (ACKR1-NC-005, ACKR1-NC-006, KEL-NC-002, KIDD-NC-002) and corrected them, surfacing the need for the full audit completed the next day.

Original compilation

229 non-coding and regulatory variants across ABO, RHD, RHCE, Duffy (ACKR1), Kell (KEL), Kidd (SLC14A1), MNS (GYPA/GYPB), and Kell Other compiled by Jun Liu, MD, PhD from primary literature, ISBT allele tables, and RhesusBase.

About this resource

A research catalog of non-coding and regulatory blood group variants, compiled for the study of variant-guided base editing strategies for antigen modulation. Not a clinical or diagnostic tool.
