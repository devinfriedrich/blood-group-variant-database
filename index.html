Non-Coding & Regulatory Variants in the Rh Blood Group System

A curated, searchable catalog of promoter, UTR, splice-site, intronic, and structural variants affecting expression of the RhD and RhCE antigens in the Rh blood group system (genes RHD and RHCE), compiled from primary literature, ISBT allele tables, and RhesusBase, with gnomAD population frequencies and base-editing feasibility annotations. This is a research resource and is not intended for clinical use.

About This Resource

A research catalog of non-coding and regulatory variants in the Rh blood group system (RHD and RHCE), compiled for the study of variant-guided base editing strategies for antigen modulation. Not a clinical or diagnostic tool. See methods & disclaimers.
